# Tacx Bike for Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024

Ride your smart trainer anywhere in MSFS 2024. Your pedalling drives a road bike on the sim's roads, the trainer's
resistance follows the slope, and an Elite Sterzo steers. Rides are saved as FIT files for Strava, Garmin Connect and
the rest.

**Full guide: [spotlegame.co.uk/tacxbike](https://www.spotlegame.co.uk/tacxbike/)**: install, first ride, the in-game
panel, keys, routes, troubleshooting.

This repository only hosts the downloads (see [Releases](https://github.com/joao12021996/tacx-bike-releases/releases)).
It's a test build: please report problems with the log files described in the guide's
[Help](https://www.spotlegame.co.uk/tacxbike/help/) page.

## Download

| | |
|---|---|
| **Installer** (bike, toolbar panel, bike bridge) | [TacxBikeSetup.exe](https://github.com/joao12021996/tacx-bike-releases/releases/latest/download/TacxBikeSetup.exe) (latest version) |

Windows 10 / 11, MSFS 2024 (Steam or Microsoft Store), Bluetooth, an FTMS smart trainer. Close the sim before installing.
The installer isn't code-signed yet: if Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

The add-on stays dormant unless you pick the Tacx Road Bike, so you never need to turn it off to fly.

## Ready-made route scenery

The sim is made to be seen from the air, so roads up close can be missing, buried or blocked. These packs add a smooth
road (flattened terrain, trees, buildings and water kept off it) and signs along the route. Each zip has the Community
packages, the route's GPX and a README.

| Route | Where | Length | Download |
|---|---|---|---|
| KVR Trail (Kettle Valley Rail Trail) | British Columbia, Canada: Hope to Castlegar | 650 km, 8,200 m climbing | [KVR-Trail-scenery.zip](https://github.com/joao12021996/tacx-bike-releases/releases/download/scenery-kvr-trail/KVR-Trail-scenery.zip) |
| Ecopista do Corgo | Northern Portugal: Peso da Régua to Chaves | 97 km, 1,500 m climbing | [Ecopista-do-Corgo-scenery.zip](https://github.com/joao12021996/tacx-bike-releases/releases/download/scenery-ecopista-do-corgo/Ecopista-do-Corgo-scenery.zip) |

Make your own from any GPX with [GPX to MSFS 2024](https://www.spotlegame.co.uk/gpx2msfs/).
